LIVESKRATCH.

Scratch live audio with the Headache Sound WOW, Serato or Traktor timecode, or an SC-1000 / SC-500, ideally through an audio interface. Use headphones if your live source is a microphone.

Works best in Chrome or Firefox: other browsers may pass only 2 channels of audio in and out, so you can’t pick an interface’s other inputs and outputs.

WOW setup

Switching the WOW from the browser needs Chrome or Edge. In other browsers, use wow/scratcher-mode.command on a Mac.
not chosen

LiveSkratch sets your own beats and samples aside on the WOW (in its LiveSkratch/ folder), loads the control tone and pilot beat, and sets the fader to cut the pilot beat. Normal puts everything back as it was, with the fader cutting the samples only so the beat always plays. Afterwards, eject the WOW and restart it: it only reads its files at start-up.

not calibrated — using nominal 1×

Saves 10 s of the raw control input, live input, output and fader as a WAV for debugging. Scratch, cut and let it ride while it records.

Tone files

The WOW setup above installs these for you. To load them by hand: the control tone goes in the WOW's samples folder, the pilot in beats. The stereo timecode is for an SC-1000 / SC-500.
